# Production access policy

Northstar Demo Organization uses least-privilege access for the reference platform. Routine production access requires an approved role and multi-factor authentication.

Emergency break-glass access requires approval from the incident commander and one platform owner. Access expires after sixty minutes. Every activation creates an audit event and must be reviewed on the next business day.

Shared accounts are prohibited. Service identities must be scoped to one component and may not be reused across the model gateway, retrieval service, and observability stack.
